### About the Lab

The Language and Cognition Lab (LaCL) is an experimental linguistics lab directed by Professor Charles Lin at Indiana University, Bloomington. LaCL conducts research on the cognitive aspects of language, focusing on sentence and discourse processing, lexical access, phonological awareness, tone processing, second language acquisition, translations and bilingualism. We are also interested in the interrelation between language and general cognitive capacities such as logic, reasoning, mathematics, space, and music, and the implications of psycholinguistic research as the scientific basis for promoting reading and literacy. We strive to provide a diverse and inclusive learning environment, and we make conscious efforts to connect our research to promoting an equal and inclusive society.
