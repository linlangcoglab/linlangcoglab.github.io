Yutong Feng (冯语彤) is a Ph.D. student in Chinese Linguistics, with a minor in Second Language Studies. Her research interests include psycholinguistics in L2 processing, usage-based language models, and Chinese as a second language. Her current research focuses on the real-time processing of numeral classifiers in L2 Chinese using eye-tracking methods. Yutong holds a master’s degree in Chinese Language and Culture, with a focus on Chinese as a Second Language, from the University of Massachusetts Amherst. She has over five years of experience teaching Chinese at the college level. Email: yf10@iu.edu
