Hongyu (Ruby) Zhang is a fourth-year PhD student in Second Language Studies. Her research interests lie at the intersection of psycholinguistics, multilingualism, and language perception and processing. She is particularly interested in how knowing and using multiple languages shapes the way people perceive and process language, as well as how social factors may influence these processes. Outside of research, Ruby enjoys photography, baking and hiking.
