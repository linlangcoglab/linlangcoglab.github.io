Anwei Yu (余安維) is a Ph.D. student in East Asian Languages and Cultures (Linguistics Track). His research interests include utilizing experimental methods to examine the cognitive processing of implied meaning in Chinese and Japanese by native speakers and learners, in addition to lexical semantics, experimental pragmatics, and applications of linguistics research and GenAI in educational settings.
