Irene Huang (Yu-Jui Huang 黃于芮) is a Ph.D. student in Chinese Linguistics at Indiana University Bloomington. Her research interests include cognitive linguistics, psycholinguistics, Chinese semantics, and the application of linguistics to Chinese language teaching. She is particularly interested in how linguistic and cognitive factors relate to language processing and production. She enjoys traveling, listening to music, and watching movies.
